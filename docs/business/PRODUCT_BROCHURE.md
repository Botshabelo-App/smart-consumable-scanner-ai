# Smart Consumable Scanner AI — Product Brochure

**Status:** Release Candidate 1 (RC1) — Pilot Evaluation and Maintenance Mode.

## Inspect smarter, not harder

Smart Consumable Scanner AI turns any smartphone into an intelligent inspection assistant for consumable products.

### What it does

- **Snaps a photo** of food, drinks, packaged goods, or raw products.
- **Detects the product** and its category automatically.
- **Analyses visible condition** using real AI — discolouration, mould, bruising, swelling, leaks, damage.
- **Reads barcodes and QR codes** to compare printed expiry data with the AI assessment.
- **Flags discrepancies** for human review.
- **Generates professional reports** in PDF, CSV, or Excel with signatures and QR codes.

### Built for South Africa and beyond

- Localised for 11 South African languages.
- Works offline in areas with poor connectivity and syncs when back online.
- Supports schools, supermarkets, warehouses, manufacturers, restaurants, hotels, and government inspectors.
- Scales from a single inspector to an enterprise with branches, devices, and role-based access.

### Trust and transparency

- Every AI result includes a confidence score and explainable findings.
- Inspectors can accept or override the AI and record a reason.
- Every inspection, override, model promotion, and report is audited.
- No fabricated results: the system uses real inference and clearly states its camera-only limitations.

### Deployment options

- **Cloud:** Docker Compose or Kubernetes on AWS, Azure, or on-premise infrastructure.
- **Mobile:** Android and iOS via React Native + Expo.
- **Backend:** FastAPI + PostgreSQL.
- **AI:** Python + PyTorch / torchvision + YOLOv8.

### Current availability

RC1 is ready for controlled pilot deployments. Organisations interested in joining the pilot program should contact the project owner. Version 1.0.0 will follow successful pilot validation and an independent AI validation review.
